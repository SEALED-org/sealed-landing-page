## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-RT | Tampering | Footer external `<a>` (Instagram/X) | mitigate | Add `rel="noopener noreferrer"` (+ `target="_blank"`) so the opened tab cannot reach `window.opener` (reverse-tabnabbing). |
| T-05-404 | Denial of Service (availability) | privacy.html/terms.html in production | mitigate | Move both into `public/` so they ship in `dist/`; post-build assertion confirms presence (Task 1). A 404 on the linked legal pages is an availability/compliance failure at launch. |
| T-05-IL | Information Disclosure | static HTML content | accept | These pages are public legal text and a public contact email by design; no secret content. The email string change is cosmetic, not a secret. |
| T-05-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | n/a | No package installs in this plan. Phase 5 installs zero new packages (RESEARCH §Standard Stack); the dist/ secret-leak gate is built in Plan 04 and runs at deploy. |
</threat_model>
