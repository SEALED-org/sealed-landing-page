| T-05-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | n/a | No package installs in this plan. The build runs `npm install` of the already-locked deps (no new packages — RESEARCH §Standard Stack); the `build:check` leak gate is the supply-chain-adjacent control and runs every deploy. |
