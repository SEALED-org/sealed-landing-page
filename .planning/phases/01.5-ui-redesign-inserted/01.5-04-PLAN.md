<objective>
Create two new sibling components that together implement the hero-form ↔ success-card swap pattern: `src/components/WaitlistForm.tsx` (renders the pill email input + black mono CTA, with loading state) and `src/components/WaitlistSuccessCard.tsx` (renders the animated white card with checkmark stamp + bobbing-arrow CTA). Neither component owns the try/catch/finally — that stays with App.tsx's `handleSubscribe` per D-06.

Purpose: The visual contract for "submit → success" in the prototype is a CSS-driven swap, not a Motion-driven AnimatePresence. The form gets `.is-submitted` (which the global rule hides via `display: none`) and the card gets `.is-mounted` (which flips `display: block`) plus `.is-visible` (which triggers the 550ms opacity/translateY transition AND the `wls-stamp` keyframe on the checkmark AND the always-on `wls-bob` keyframe on the down-arrow). This plan defines the JSX shapes and the prop contracts; the CSS rules already exist in `src/index.css` from Plan 01.5-02; the wiring in App.tsx happens in Plan 01.5-09.

Output: Two new files. No edits to App.tsx in this plan.
</objective>
